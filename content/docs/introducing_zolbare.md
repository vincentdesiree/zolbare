Zolbare is a lightweight theme for [Zola](https://www.getzola.org/), built for people who want a clean, readable website without a pile of JavaScript or unnecessary complexity.

The idea is simple: keep the focus on your content, make the theme easy to customize, and let Zola handle the heavy lifting.

## What you get

* **Responsive layout** that adapts to desktop and mobile screens.
* **Light and dark color schemes** for comfortable reading.
* **Table of contents** for navigating longer articles.
* **Tags and series** to organize your content.
* **Code blocks** with syntax highlighting support.
* **Optional page elements** for links, widgets, and other useful extras.

## Why Zolbare?

No frontend framework to maintain, no JavaScript dependency for the core layout, and no complicated setup just to publish a page.

Zolbare is designed to stay out of your way. Start with the defaults, customize what you need, and keep the rest simple.
